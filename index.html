FluxNodes Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

FluxNodes ("FluxNodes", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile puzzle game for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use FluxNodes and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

FluxNodes does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment information, photos, messages, or other user-generated content.

FluxNodes uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertising. As a result, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect information associated with advertising and app usage, including:

  • Device identifiers or similar device information
  • Advertising information, such as ads displayed or interacted with
  • App and product interaction information, such as app launches and interactions
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general/coarse location
  • Crash and performance information related to the advertising SDK

The information collected through third-party advertising services is handled according to the applicable policies of those providers and applicable privacy laws.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through the advertising SDK may be used for purposes including:

  • Displaying third-party advertisements
  • Measuring and improving advertising performance
  • Detecting fraud and maintaining advertising security
  • Improving the performance and reliability of advertising services

FluxNodes itself does not use precise location information and does not require access to your device's precise location.

3. Third-Party Advertising

FluxNodes uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and process information from the app for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms website.

4. Tracking and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with consent or privacy choices concerning advertising and tracking. On iOS, Apple may also provide App Tracking Transparency controls where applicable.

You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your device settings and, where available, the privacy or consent options provided by the advertising service.

5. Data Retention

FluxNodes does not maintain a user account database containing the advertising information collected by AdMob. Retention of information collected by third-party advertising services is determined by the relevant provider's policies and applicable legal requirements.

6. Data Sharing

FluxNodes does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared by third-party service providers, including Google and its advertising partners, when necessary to provide advertising and related services, subject to their policies and applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

FluxNodes does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and data processing are subject to the settings, consent requirements, and protections applicable to the user's age and location.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by FluxNodes. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of your personal information. Because much of the information described in this policy is collected and processed by third-party advertising providers, requests concerning that information may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this location with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or FluxNodes' privacy practices, you can contact us through the support contact provided on the FluxNodes App Store product page.
